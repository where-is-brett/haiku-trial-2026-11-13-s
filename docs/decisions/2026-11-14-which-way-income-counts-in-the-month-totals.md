# Which way income counts in the month totals

- **Date:** 14 November 2026
- **Piece:** month-page
- **Options:** A. Use the sign as the statement gives it. Money in and money back both read negative, so income counts negative (No extra work. Totals follow the statement's own signs; Can be undone) · B. Negate income as the design says. This is right only if the statement gives income as a plus amount (No extra work. The grand total is wrong if the statement gives income as a minus amount; Can be undone)
- **Recommended:** keep
- **Chosen:** keep
- **The director's words:** none
